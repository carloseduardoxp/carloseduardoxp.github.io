# About Me

I am a Software Engineering Professor at the [Federal Institute of Science and Technology at Triângulo Mineiro (IFTM)](https://iftm.edu.br/), in Uberlândia/MG since 2014, and hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the [Federal University of Uberlândia](http://www.ufu.br/) (UFU, 2025). I received my Masters degree in Computer Science from UFU (2017) and my Bachelors degree in Computer Engineering from the [State University of Minas Gerais](https://www.uemg.br/ituiutaba) (UEMG, 2005). I also hold a specialization in Java Development from [UNITRI](https://unitri.edu.br/) (2007) and the Sun Certified Java Programmer certification (SCJP, 2007). Before entering academia, I accumulated nine years of professional experience as a software developer in the industry (2006 to 2014). My main research interests include software repository mining for program comprehension and recommendation systems for software development.

For more detailed information, you can refer to [my lattes CV, which contains additional information in Portuguese-BR](http://lattes.cnpq.br/9157131878203641).
